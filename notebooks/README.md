# 🧬 BiorXiv Venture Intelligence Suite
An automated, data-driven bioinformatics and venture intelligence pipeline designed to bridge systems biology with commercial de-risking for biotech venture capital and drug discovery.
---
## 🚀 Overview
Evaluating early-stage biological targets traditionally requires manual cross-referencing of metabolic flux profiles, historical clinical trial failures, and proprietary target novelty metrics. 
The BiorXiv Venture Intelligence Suite automates this end-to-end evaluation. It scans underexplored ("dark") proteomes, runs constraint-based metabolic optimizations (Human Recon3D), and synthesizes quantitative findings directly into executive investment memos.
---
## 🛠️ Core Architecture & Tech Stack
* **optimizer.py**: Performs constraint-based metabolic flux optimization on Recon3D subsets to evaluate target viability against environmental constraints.
* **main.py / engine.py**: Executes automated dark proteome target scanning, indexing structural novelty against historical clinical trial attrition metrics.
* **memo_generator.py**: Translates raw algorithmic outputs into formatted executive investment briefs (venture_memo.md) for venture decision support.
